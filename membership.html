Frequently Asked Questions

Who is eligible to join?

Our community is designed for senior executives either working in or transitioning to fractional roles. We focus exclusively on C-suite and senior leadership positions. And while we love our recruiter friends (please keep sending us jobs!), this community is for practitioners only.

What type of opportunities are shared in the community?

The FEC gets access to a pipeline of both exclusive and curated job leads. Our opportunities are exclusively senior leadership fractional roles across various industries. Members get first access to these positions before they're shared elsewhere.

How active is the community?

Our members are engaged professionals who actively share insights, opportunities, and experiences. We host regular events and encourage daily interaction through our platform, while respecting that everyone manages their own time and engagement level.

What happens after I apply?

We carefully review all applications to maintain the high caliber of our community. If successful, we aim to respond within 48 hours.

Is there a refund policy?

Sorry, no. Caveat emptor. We're all experienced professionals here - you get out of it what you put in, just like any community.

Do you offer any financial assistance?

Yes, we believe everyone should have access to the resources they need to succeed. If the membership fee would prevent you from joining, please inquire about our Community Fellowship Program.
