Ever wondered how a fractional exec can help you?

March 9, 2024 • By Zachary King

If you're a startup founder or scaling entrepreneur, you've probably heard the buzz about fractional executives. But maybe you're still wondering, "What exactly can they do for my business?"

Well, I've put together a table to help answer that question for you. It's compiled with real-world examples of how fractional execs have made a tangible impact across various industries and roles. This list is dynamic - we'll be constantly updating it with fresh examples as our community of fractional execs continues to share.

Let's take a peek at some of the ways these experienced professionals have helped businesses like yours:

  • A Fractional VP Sales built an outbound enterprise sales motion for a B2B2C-allied health marketplace.
  • A Fractional CTO helped an AI-driven note-taking SaaS startup transition between an exiting founder and hiring of a full-time CTO.
  • A Fractional CMO upskilled a team of 100+ strategists, creatives and project managers on how to build and run a successful ABM program.
  • A Fractional HR Executive designed, restructured and headhunted key exec and leadership talent for a major business transformation.

These are just a few examples from our ever-expanding table. From sales and marketing to tech and HR, fractional execs bring a wealth of experience to the table - without the full-time price tag.

They're not just consultants who advise and leave. These professionals roll up their sleeves and get hands-on with your business challenges, providing targeted expertise exactly when and where you need it most.

So, whether you're looking to boost revenue, streamline operations, or navigate a tricky transition period, a fractional exec could be the secret weapon you've been searching for.

Curious to learn more? Take a look at our dynamic table of mini use cases. It might just spark some ideas on how a fractional exec could help take your business to the next level. And keep checking back - you never know what new insights and examples you might find as we continue to update it.

Remember, in the fast-paced world of startups and scale-ups, having the right expertise at the right time can make all the difference.
