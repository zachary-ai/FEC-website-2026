What is a Fractional VP Sales?

January 19, 2023 • By Zachary King

The fractional executive movement has expanded rapidly in recent years. A Fractional executive is someone who fills a senior executive position in a part-time or short-term capacity, usually 5-25 hours per week. These professionals typically occupy VP-level or C-suite roles and provide targeted expertise without the expense of a full-time hire.

For emerging companies, this model offers significant advantages. Rather than allocating substantial portions of funding toward a single executive salary, startups can access experienced leadership strategically.

Three Key Market Drivers

Three tailwinds are supporting this movement:

  1. Available talent - Recent layoffs have created a pool of experienced professionals
  2. Profitability focus - Companies now prioritize runway management over growth-at-all-costs strategies
  3. Lifestyle flexibility - Professionals increasingly seek alternatives to demanding full-time positions

Fractional Work vs. Consulting

A critical distinction exists between these models. The fractional approach involves "hands-on operational" involvement—not advisory-only engagement. These professionals manage teams, implement changes, conduct hiring, and handle business development directly.

Fractional VP Sales Responsibilities

Strategic Areas Include:

  • Go-to-market strategy development
  • Sales process and methodology design
  • Value proposition refinement
  • Playbook creation
  • Team structure and KPI planning
  • Technology stack evaluation

Operational Responsibilities Encompass:

  • Team management and coaching
  • Business development activities
  • Sales hiring and interviewing
  • Forecasting
  • Skills training
  • Account planning

When Organizations Need This Role

Companies benefit when they require sales expertise but cannot justify $350k+ (plus benefits, options, etc) for a full-time position. This applies to startups wanting to preserve capital or founders who've reached bandwidth limitations.
