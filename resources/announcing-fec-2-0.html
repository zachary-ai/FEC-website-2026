Announcing FEC 2.0!

November 23, 2024 • By Zachary King

The Fractional Exec community is launching FEC 2.0, a new platform replacing our existing Slack community.

What's New

We've completed "The Ultimate Guide to Becoming a Fractional Executive" - a comprehensive 10,000-word playbook containing strategies, examples, and templates for building a fractional practice.

Member Benefits

The new platform provides members with:

  • The complete fractional executive guide
  • Access to a searchable community of 650+ active fractional executives
  • Curated job opportunities exclusive to members
  • Hands-on workshops and skill-building sessions
  • Virtual and live events
  • Partner discounts on business tools

Membership Options

Three subscription options are available:

  • 6 months: $240
  • 12 months: $399
  • Lifetime: $599

Timeline

The platform launches November 25th, with the Slack community closing December 11th. Early adopters receive founding member rates.

Our Growth

We've grown to 900+ fractional executives since 2022, exceeding the capacity of our previous infrastructure. FEC 2.0 is built to scale with our community.
