# Part 6: Commercials (ZacBot Edition)

## Pricing Frameworks

### Salary Breakdown Method
Last salary + 40% (benefits/super/leave) / number of clients = per-client rate.
Example: $250K + 40% = $350K / 3 clients = ~$10K/month per client. This is your floor.

### Lifestyle Math
Monthly needs + savings goals + business expenses + downtime buffer (20-30%) / target clients.
Example: $15K/month + $5K expenses = $20K/month / 3 clients = ~$7K minimum per client.

### Market Benchmarks (Australia)

| Company Stage | Typical Monthly Range |
|---|---|
| Early-stage startups | $5-8K/month |
| Growth stage | $8-12K/month |
| Enterprise | $12-15K+/month |

Rates vary by function (CFOs/CTOs command higher), location (Sydney/Melbourne pay more), and experience.

## Five Pricing Models

1. **Full Retainer:** $5-15K/month, 1-2 days/week. Bread and butter of fractional work.
2. **Workshop:** $2-5K entry-level. Relationship starter and qualification tool.
3. **Project:** $10-30K, 4-12 weeks. Fixed scope, clear deliverables and endpoint.
4. **Advisory:** $1-3K/month. Regular calls, strategic sounding board, lower intensity.
5. **Tiered Approach:** Workshop -> project -> retainer -> advisory. Each step proves value.

### The Reverse Pipeline
Use diagnostic workshops as top-of-funnel. A paid workshop ($2-5K) surfaces problems you solve. It's both revenue AND a sales tool. Conversion from workshop to retainer is significantly higher than from cold outreach.

### Pricing Principles
- Better 2 clients at $10K than 4 at $5K (context switching cost)
- Don't discount to win work, it sets a hard-to-undo precedent
- If you're closing every deal, you're probably too cheap
- Review rates annually
- Walk away if numbers don't work

## Legal Structure (Australia)

**Sole Trader:** Fastest, cheapest, personal liability. Fine for testing.

**Pty Ltd:** $500-700 setup, ~$300/yr ASIC fees, asset protection, more tax options. Where most end up.

**Trust:** Family tax planning, better asset protection, more complex, higher costs.

**Practical path:** Start sole trader, switch to Pty Ltd with traction.

### Essential Registrations
1. ABN
2. Business name (if not personal name)
3. GST registration (mandatory over $75K)
4. PAYG if employing yourself
5. TFN for business structure

### Key Documents
- **Service Agreement:** Scope, fees, payment terms, IP, confidentiality, termination
- **Statement of Work:** Per-engagement deliverables, timeline, success criteria
- **Advisor Agreement:** For lighter arrangements
- **NDA/Confidentiality:** Template ready for clients who want it

Get a lawyer to review your standard agreement once. Few hundred dollars well spent.

## Insurance

- **Professional Indemnity:** $2M minimum (non-negotiable for strategic advisory work)
- **Public Liability:** $10-20M (some clients require it)
- **Cyber Insurance:** If handling sensitive data
- **Cost:** ~$1-2K/year
- Have Certificate of Currency ready, removes friction from sales process

## How to Sell Yourself

### Reframe
You're not selling. You're diagnosing whether you can help with a problem they already have.

**Sales conversation pattern:**
1. Understand their situation (listen more than talk)
2. Diagnose the problem (show you get it)
3. Explain your approach (share your framework)
4. Propose a starting point (usually a workshop or small project)

### Discovery Call (30-45 min)
**Cover:**
- What's happening in the business now?
- Biggest challenge or opportunity?
- What have they tried?
- What does success look like in 6 months?
- Budget and timeline?
- Who's involved in the decision?

**Key:** Ask great questions and listen. If you can articulate their challenge better than they can, you've already won.

### Proposals
Keep to 1-2 pages max:
- Here's what I heard (their problem in their words)
- Here's what I recommend (your approach)
- Here's what it costs (clear pricing)
- Here's what happens next (specific next step)

### Referral Engine

**The 10% Rule:** Fractionals pay each other 10% referral fees. Someone brings you work, give them 10%. You send work, earn 10%. Normalize this.

**Scripts:**
- After failed discovery: "Since you understand what I do, who else faces this problem?"
- After a win: "I'm looking to work with more companies facing [challenge]. Anyone in your network?"
- Capacity ask: "I've got capacity for one more client this quarter. Looking for [ICP]."

**Make it easy:** Draft the intro email for them.

**Best timing:** After wins, after praise, after project completion, even after a "no."

**Systematize:** Add referral ask to engagement closeout, quarterly nurture outreach, treat as a system not an afterthought.

### Negotiation

- **Know your walk-away number** before any pricing conversation
- **Anchor high, flex on structure:** Don't drop rate, change scope. Instead of $10K to $8K, offer $10K for 1 day instead of 1.5 days.
- **Tiered entry:** If they balk at $10K/month, offer $3K workshop first
- **Never negotiate against yourself:** State your price. Shut up. Let them respond.
- **Say no to bad fits.** Your reputation is built on engagements you take AND decline.

## Financial Management

- Have 6 months runway before going full fractional
- Know your exact monthly burn rate
- Separate accounts for: operating expenses, tax (25-30%), GST, your pay, profit
- Keep personal and business completely separate
- Get a good accountant early

### Cash Flow Reality
Fractional income is lumpy. Clients end, replacements take time. Normal.
- Invoice promptly (don't wait for month end)
- Set payment terms at 7 days, not 30
- Keep tax money separate (ATO doesn't care about your cash flow)
- Maintain pipeline even when fully booked

Setup cost: less than $5K if done right.
