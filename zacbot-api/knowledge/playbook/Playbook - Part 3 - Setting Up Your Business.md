# Part 3: Setting Up Your Business (ZacBot Edition)

*Australia-focused, but principles apply internationally. Swap structures and regulatory bodies for your local equivalents.*

## Business Structure (Australia)

**Sole Trader:** Fastest/cheapest, ABN takes 5 minutes, personal liability (assets at risk). Fine for starting out.

**Pty Ltd:** $500-700 setup, ~$300/year ASIC fees, protects personal assets, more tax planning options. Where most successful fractionals end up.

**Trust Structure:** Good for family tax planning, better asset protection, more flexible profit distribution, higher annual costs, more complex.

**Practical path:** Start sole trader to test the waters. Switch to Pty Ltd once you have traction.

## Essential Registrations

1. ABN (Australian Business Number)
2. Business name (if not using your personal name)
3. GST registration (mandatory over $75K, you'll hit this fast)
4. PAYG if employing yourself
5. TFN for your business structure

## Insurance

- Professional indemnity: $2M minimum (non-negotiable)
- Public liability: $10-20M
- Cyber insurance: if handling sensitive data
- Typical cost: $1-2K/year for comprehensive coverage
- Have your Certificate of Currency ready, some clients require it before engaging

## Banking

- Separate business account (any major bank)
- Dedicated business debit card
- Wise for international payments
- High-interest savings for tax money
- Keep personal and business completely separate from day one

## Accounting & Tax

- Get a good accountant early
- Accounting system: Xero (most popular), MYOB, or QuickBooks
- Set aside 25-30% for tax from every payment
- Track all expenses from day one, keep all receipts

## Essential Tech Stack

**Core business tools:**
- Google Workspace (~$9/month)
- Xero for accounting
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator for lead gen
- Scheduling tool (SavvyCal or similar) for managing multiple calendars
- Qwilr for professional proposals

**AI stack (non-negotiable):**
- ChatGPT Plus ($20/month) for content and general tasks
- Claude for technical writing and complex analysis
- Perplexity for research

Build your own workflows and prompt library. The magic is in how you use them.

**Scaling yourself:**
- AI: process docs, content frameworks, automated tasks, first drafts, research
- Human: offshore VA for admin, specialist contractors, network of trusted freelancers

## Documents & Templates

Essential templates to customize:
- Service Agreement
- Advisor Agreement
- Statement of Work
- Discovery questionnaire
- Weekly update template

## First Week Checklist

1. Register business number
2. Choose business structure
3. Set up business account
4. Get basic insurance
5. Set up accounting system
6. Set up core tech stack
7. Customize document templates
8. Set up professional email

## Financial Setup

- Have 6 months runway if possible
- Know your monthly burn rate exactly
- Set up separate accounts for: operating expenses, tax (25-30%), GST, your pay, profit (yes, from day one)

## Red Flags to Avoid

- Spending too much on branding before you have clients
- Over-investing in tech stack
- Mixing personal/business money
- Skipping insurance
- Not getting legal review of templates
- Working from personal email

Successful fractionals typically spend less than $5K getting set up properly. If you're spending more, you're overthinking it.
