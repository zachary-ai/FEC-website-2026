# Personal Branding for Fractional Executives: Workshop Summary (ZacBot Edition)

**Host:** Zac King | **Guest:** Vinisha, MD of P3 Partners, Author of "The Briefcase Effect"

## Key Takeaways

### 1. Define What You Want to Be Known For
- Identify your "pursuit of mastery," the thread running through everything you do
- Your personal brand should survive a job change. If you only identify with your title, you're exposed.
- Pick 2-3 things you want people to associate with you
- Start building while still employed, don't wait until you need it

### 2. Build Your Online Presence (The Bar Is Low)
- LinkedIn is your 24/7 resume, treat it as social proofing
- Start by sharing others' content with your own take before creating original
- Write about what you're learning and thinking, insights obvious to you after 20 years aren't obvious to everyone
- Talk to your audience of two years ago, what would have helped you then?
- Don't be discouraged by low engagement. 20 impressions from your ICP beats 3,000 random ones.

### 3. Stay Human in the Age of AI
- Creativity, problem solving, and real connection are what AI can't replicate
- Real beats polished. Typos feel human.
- Build rituals people look forward to (e.g., "Non-AI Friday")
- Only share what's relevant to what you want to be known for

### 4. Build Relationships, Not Transactions
- Choose character over titles when networking
- Go outside your usual circle, connect across industries
- Don't wait until you need something to build relationships
- Follow up after events. In-person + online follow-up is the most powerful combination.
- Don't force connections. Be professional and polite, but don't fake it.

### 5. Manage Multiple Brands With One Theme
- Multiple offerings (consulting, speaking, writing) need a consistent thread underneath
- Lack of consistency disables trust
- Shift context, not character. Ask: would people still recognise what I stand for if I changed roles tomorrow?

### 6. Repurpose and Recycle Content
- Publish longer pieces as LinkedIn articles, repost every 3 months
- Cross-post to your website for full control
- Bucket content by pain point, not chronologically
- Think creative partnerships for distribution
