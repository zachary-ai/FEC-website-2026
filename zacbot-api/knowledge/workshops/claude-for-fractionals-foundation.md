# Claude For Fractionals: Build Your Foundation (ZacBot Edition)

**Sprint Week 1 | Goal:** Teach Claude who you are
**Time:** 2-3 hours total
**Output:** Your "Foundational Context" document

## The Concept

Stop prompting Claude cold. Build context docs about who you are, then let Claude interview you to fill gaps. This is your "Constitution," the foundational context shaping everything Claude does for you.

### Three Layers of Context

| Layer | Covers | Why It Matters |
|-------|--------|---------------|
| Constitution | Who you are, values | Claude understands your lens |
| Work | What you do, how, what's working | Claude can operate in your business |
| Goals | Where you're headed | Claude can help you get there |

**Payoff:** You can give Claude a task, go work out, come back to a solved problem. Only works if Claude has the context.

## Step 1: Gather Existing Docs (30 min)

Create a `Context` folder. Dump everything you have:
- Business plan, positioning docs, service offerings, pricing
- Website copy, LinkedIn profile text, brochures, case studies
- Proposals, SOWs, contracts, discovery call notes, testimonials
- CV/resume, bios, speaking descriptions
- Goals, OKRs, annual planning docs

Don't overthink it. If it describes any part of your business, throw it in.

## Step 2: Have Claude Read Everything (15 min)

Open Claude Code in the folder: "Read everything. Give me a summary of what you understand, what's clear vs unclear, gaps/questions, and any contradictions."

## Step 3: Run the Interview (60-90 min)

Claude asks questions one at a time covering:
1. Background and how you got here
2. Actual work, deliverables, outcomes
3. What you explicitly DON'T do
4. Ideal clients, triggers for hiring you
5. Positioning, what makes you different
6. Pricing, engagement types, work structure
7. Goals for next 6-12 months
8. Working preferences, tools, communication style
9. Values, non-negotiables
10. Anything else needed for deep understanding

Tips: Don't rush. If you don't have a clear answer, that's useful. Be honest about what's working and what isn't. Include the messy stuff.

## Step 4: Review and Refine (30 min)

Review the output:
- Does it sound like you?
- Anything wrong or outdated?
- What's missing?
Ask Claude to revise until it feels right.

## Output Structure

1-3 page document covering:
- Who I Am (background, why fractional, past experience)
- What I Do (core service, deliverables, what I'm known for)
- What I Don't Do (boundaries, referral areas, red flags)
- Ideal Clients (industry, stage, size, triggers, fit criteria)
- Positioning (differentiation, unique angle, why clients choose me)
- Business Model (pricing, engagement types, scoping approach)
- Goals (6-12 month direction, success definition)
- Working Style (communication, tools, preferences)
- Values (non-negotiables, decision-making principles)

## Saving Your Foundation

Save as `FOUNDATION.md` at business folder root, in a `Context/` folder, or referenced in your `CLAUDE.md`. Key: available whenever Claude Code starts in your business folder.

## Pro Tip: Autonomous Workflow

Once foundation is built:
1. Give Claude a task (~60 min of work)
2. Turn on auto-accept (Shift+Tab)
3. Go do something else
4. Come back to a solved problem

Only works if Claude has context. Without foundational docs, Claude spins asking clarifying questions. With them, it executes.
