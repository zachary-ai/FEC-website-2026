# Fractional Exec Community (FEC) Overview

## What FEC Is

The Fractional Exec Community is a paid operator community for experienced fractional executives. It was founded by Zac King as part of Fractional Enterprises, a dual-engine business combining hands-on Fractional GTM work with the community.

FEC is not a generic networking group. It's a community of real operators - people who have run sales, marketing, ops, and product teams and are now fractional or transitioning to fractional.

## Who It's For

- Experienced operators (sales, marketing, ops, product) who are already fractional or transitioning
- People with real operational experience, not first-time consultants
- Globally distributed, English-speaking
- Operator mindset, not consultant mindset

## What Members Get

- **Peer network:** Real operators sharing playbooks, challenges, wins, and introductions
- **Practical playbooks and templates:** GTM frameworks, AI-powered systems templates, proposal structures, client management workflows
- **Live workshops and AMAs:** Deep dives on AI for fractionals, personal branding, sales playbook design, and more
- **GTM and sales education:** Grounded in real client work, with AI and automation integration
- **Job and opportunity flow:** Client referrals from the community, partnership opportunities
- **Tool discounts and partnerships:** Negotiated discounts on tools for fractional practices
- **Direct access to Zac's frameworks:** Current thinking on AI-enabled GTM, behind-the-scenes of real work

## The Platform

FEC runs on Mighty Networks (not Slack). Members access it via browser or dedicated mobile app (iOS/Android). It has a Facebook-like interface with posts, comments, DMs, a dedicated Jobs channel, and topic-based spaces.

## Key Problems FEC Solves

1. **Positioning and pricing:** How to position fractional vs consultant, what to charge, how to structure engagements
2. **Consistent pipeline:** Generating steady opportunities beyond referrals, building personal brand for inbound
3. **Operating across multiple clients:** Managing 2-4 concurrent engagements without burning out
4. **Avoiding contractor dynamics:** Being a strategic partner, not a task executor
5. **Scaling personal leverage:** Building systems, IP, and AI automation to work smarter
