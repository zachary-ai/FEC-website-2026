# Pattern Library Starter Template

A compound learning system for fractional executives. Each engagement makes the next one better.

---

## How to Use This Template

1. Create a `Patterns/` directory in your working folder
2. Copy the category files below (or start with just one)
3. After each client engagement, extract 2-3 patterns using the extraction prompt at the bottom
4. Add them to the relevant category file
5. Reference your patterns when starting new engagements

**The principle:** Your 10th client gets a better playbook than your 1st, and it takes half the time.

---

## Pattern Format

Every pattern follows this structure:

```
### [Pattern Name]
**Source:** [Client/engagement where you discovered this]
**Context:** [The situation that led to this insight]
**Insight:** [The pattern itself, 1-3 sentences]
**Application:** [How to use this in future engagements]
**Evidence:** [Data or outcomes that validate the pattern]
```

## Category 1: ICP Patterns

Patterns about ideal customer profiles, segmentation, and targeting.

### [Example] The Hidden Buyer
**Source:** [Client A, Q1 2026]
**Context:** Enterprise deals were stalling at the C-suite level despite strong initial interest.
**Insight:** The actual decision-maker was two levels below the C-suite. Executive Assistants and Ops leads drove 23% of enterprise revenue at 6x the deal size of CEO-sourced deals.
**Application:** Always map the buying committee. Target the operational buyer, not just the economic buyer.
**Evidence:** 23% of revenue, 6x average deal size vs CEO-sourced.

---

## Category 2: Messaging Patterns

Patterns about positioning, value props, and communication that converts.

### [Example] The Breakup Email
**Source:** [Client B, Q2 2026]
**Context:** Prospects going dark after 2-3 follow-ups. Standard nurture sequences getting no response.
**Insight:** "Should I close your file?" breakup email consistently gets the highest response rate of any template. The scarcity trigger works when value-add follow-ups don't.
**Application:** Add a breakup email at position 4-5 in every outbound sequence. Keep it short. Make the ask clear.
**Evidence:** Highest response rate of any single template across 3 client engagements.

---

## Category 3: Outbound Patterns

Patterns about prospecting, lead generation, and pipeline building.

### [Example] The Competitive Angle
**Source:** [Client C, Q3 2026]
**Context:** Generic outbound was getting 1-2% reply rates. Needed a differentiated approach.
**Insight:** Campaigns targeting competitor customers with specific switching triggers achieved 6.9% reply rate, 4-5x industry standard.
**Application:** Identify top 3 competitors. Map their known weaknesses. Build campaigns around switching moments (contract renewals, product gaps, pricing changes).
**Evidence:** 6.9% reply rate vs 1.5% industry average.

---

## Category 4: Analysis Patterns

Patterns about data analysis, diagnostics, and insight extraction.

### [Example] Domain-Based Segmentation
**Source:** [Client A, Q1 2026]
**Context:** CRM data was messy. Needed a quick way to segment 5,000+ contacts by industry without manual tagging.
**Insight:** Email domain predicts industry at 80%+ accuracy for B2B contacts. Government (.gov), education (.edu), and corporate domains cluster reliably.
**Application:** Before running any outbound campaign, segment the contact list by email domain. Quick proxy for industry when CRM data is unreliable.
**Evidence:** 80%+ accuracy across 3 client CRM exports.

---

## Category 5: Handoff Patterns

Patterns about team transitions, documentation, and sustainable hand-offs.

### [Example] The SOP-Driven Handoff
**Source:** [Client B, Q2 2026]
**Context:** Founder was spending 15+ hours/week on sales tasks. Needed to hand off to a junior hire without losing quality.
**Insight:** SOP-driven handoff with video walkthroughs reduced founder involvement from 15h/wk to 2h/wk within 14 weeks. Key: document the decision logic, not just the steps.
**Application:** Build handoff SOPs that capture the "why" behind each decision point. Include video for complex workflows. Set a 14-week transition timeline.
**Evidence:** 15h/wk to 2h/wk in 14 weeks. Junior hire ramped to 80% autonomy by week 10.

## Extraction Prompt

Run this at the end of each engagement to extract new patterns:

```
Review the work completed for [Client Name] across all deliverables, call notes,
and outcomes. Extract reusable patterns in these categories:

1. ICP Patterns - What did we learn about targeting and segmentation?
2. Messaging Patterns - What messaging, positioning, or copy approaches worked?
3. Outbound Patterns - What outreach strategies or campaigns performed above average?
4. Analysis Patterns - What data analysis techniques revealed unexpected insights?
5. Handoff Patterns - What worked (or didn't) in team transitions and documentation?

For each pattern, provide:
- Pattern Name (concise, memorable)
- Context (the situation)
- Insight (the reusable learning)
- Application (how to use it next time)
- Evidence (data or outcomes)

Only extract patterns with clear evidence. Skip anything speculative.
```
