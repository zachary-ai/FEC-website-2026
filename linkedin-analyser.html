Common Questions

LinkedIn Tips for Fractional Executives

What should a fractional executive's LinkedIn headline say?

Your headline should clearly state your fractional role, your specialty, and the outcome you deliver. For example: "Fractional CMO | B2B SaaS Growth | Helping startups scale from $1M to $10M ARR". Avoid generic titles like "Marketing Consultant" - be specific about being fractional.

How is a fractional executive different from a consultant?

Fractional executives are embedded operators who take accountability for outcomes, not just advice. A consultant advises from the outside; a fractional executive leads from within. Fractionals typically work 1-2 days per week with each client and are accountable for KPIs and team performance.

What should I include in my LinkedIn About section as a fractional?

Focus on: (1) Who you help and what problem you solve, (2) Your track record with specific numbers, (3) How you work (fractional model explanation), (4) A clear call to action. Avoid listing skills - instead, tell a story about the outcomes you deliver for clients.

How do I attract clients through LinkedIn as a fractional executive?

The best fractional executives attract inbound leads by: sharing real insights from their work (not generic advice), being specific about results they've achieved, engaging with founders and CEOs in their target market, and positioning themselves as specialists rather than generalists.
